Before you call

Four answers turn a callback into a quote.

Most quote delays aren't pricing — they're waiting on details. If you have these, we can usually price a lane while you're still on the phone.

Dimensions, not just weight

Pallet count, footprint and stacked height. Carriers bill the space you take up, so a light, tall pallet can price above a heavy, dense one.

What's at each end

Dock, liftgate, or a residential kerb. Whether the receiver takes appointments, and the window they take them in. This is the most common cause of a re-quote.

The date that can't move

Not the ideal date — the one with a consequence attached. It decides the mode, and the mode decides most of the price.

Anything unusual about the freight

Temperature control, hazmat class, high value, awkward shape, or "do not stack." Told upfront it's a rate line. Found at the dock it's a delay.
