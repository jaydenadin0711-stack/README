Henderson & Las Vegas, NV

Started as a coffee cart. Still run like one.

Veronica Croaker began pouring cups out of a cart before Skimos had walls. Eight-plus years later there are two of them, the beans are USDA certified organic, and the person behind the counter still knows what you usually get.

Where the beans come from

Organic, and roasted by people who'd tell us if it wasn't good

The coffee is USDA certified organic, roasted by Olympia Coffee up in Washington. In 2021, iHeartRadio named Skimos Nevada's Best Coffee Shop. We bring it up more than we probably should, because a cart-to-two-storefronts business doesn't get many chances to brag.
