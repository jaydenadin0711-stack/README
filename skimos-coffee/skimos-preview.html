The short version and the long one

A cart, then a shop, then a second shop

Veronica Croaker is from Mexico City. Skimos didn't start as a business plan. It started as a coffee cart, the kind you'd wheel out and set up wherever people already were, before there was a lease or an espresso machine bolted to a counter.

Somewhere in there it worked. Not overnight, either: eight-plus years, if you're counting the way local news did when they covered the second location opening. Henderson came first. The Las Vegas shop, tucked into Vegas Pointe Plaza on South Las Vegas Boulevard, came after Henderson had already proven the cart-turned-cafe thing wasn't a fluke.

It's still a small, woman-owned shop. It just happens to have two addresses now instead of one folding table.

The coffee itself is USDA certified organic, roasted by Olympia Coffee out of Washington state, not a house-brand blend with a made-up origin story on the bag. In 2021, iHeartRadio named Skimos Nevada's Best Coffee Shop, which is the kind of thing a small operation doesn't get handed for free.

  • Early days A coffee cart, run by Veronica Croaker, no fixed address.
  • Henderson The cart becomes a storefront at 1533 W Sunset Rd.
  • 2021 Named Nevada's Best Coffee Shop by iHeartRadio.
  • Eight years in A second location opens in Vegas Pointe Plaza on South Las Vegas Boulevard.

Neither shop is trying to be a chain. The menu has the same handful of specialty drinks at both counters (the mazapán latte, the honey white mocha, the avocado toast people photograph before they eat) because that's what got regulars in the door in the first place. Changing it wouldn't make sense.
