    1. Point the camera

      SCAN turns on the back camera. The screen becomes a viewfinder with a framing box.

    2. Take the photo

      Press SCAN again or the side shutter. The flash LED fires next to the lens.

    3. Read and solve

      The photo goes over Wi‑Fi to be read. It comes back as the problem, the answer and the steps.

    4. Answer to ANS

      Scroll the steps with ▲▼. Press = and the answer drops into the calculator as ANS.

    What's simulated

    This page writes the problem on the paper itself, so it already knows how to solve it, and it works with no internet. The real SC‑1 sends the photo to Claude, which can read handwriting and word problems it has never seen.

    Keyboard

    0–9 + − * / ^ ( ) Enter = · Backspace ⌫ · Esc AC · S SCAN · F formulas · L log · P π · ↑ ↓ browse

    The formulas that come up most in Algebra 1 and Algebra 2. The same list is built into the calculator: press f(x) and browse with ▲▼, or tap Show on calculator on any card.
