GMT-MASTER II — voiceover script (30s)
Read slowly and calmly, like you're letting someone in on a secret. Leave a clear
one-second pause between lines: the mixer uses those pauses to put each line under
its caption.

 1. Some watches tell the time.                                    (0.6s)
 2. This one tells two.                                            (3.4s)
 3. Born in the cockpit, for pilots crossing the world overnight.  (6.4s)
 4. Two time zones. One glance.                                    (9.8s)
 5. Oystersteel. Five-piece Jubilee links.                         (12.8s)
 6. Sealed by the Oysterclasp.                                     (15.8s)
 7. When the lights go down, it keeps shining.                     (18.8s)
 8. Wherever you land, you're already on time.                     (21.9s)
 9. GMT-Master Two. Rolex.                                         (26.4s, on the logo)

Each line has to fit before the next one starts. Line 3 is the longest,
with about 3 seconds for it.
